Formiga Farm

Formiga Farm is where Formiga Desktop's creatures are made and remade. To install it, drag
Formiga Farm into Applications. Formiga Desktop will find it there on its own, and open it from a
companion's page in its notebook to give them a new look, or from the studio to draw a new one.

Formiga Farm only ever changes how a creature looks: never its name, its nature, its friends or
its story. Formiga Desktop decides whether to keep each new look. The creature stays on your
desktop the whole time Farm is open, and if anything goes wrong it simply keeps the look it had.
